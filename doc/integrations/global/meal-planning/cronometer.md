# Cronometer

Detailed nutrition tracker. Offers data export (CSV) for diary, nutrition and biometrics; a partner API exists for approved integrations (unverified).

## Access

- **export** - Settings > Account > Export Data: servings, daily nutrition, exercises, biometrics CSV.
- **api-partner** - Cronometer API/partner programme (unverified).

## Data

Servings with food name, amount, energy and ~80 nutrients.
