# Krónan

Icelandic supermarket chain. Its app offers Scan & Go in-store checkout, pickup/delivery ordering, reordering past purchases and recipe-to-cart ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kr%C3%B3nan/id1504695642?uo=4)).
