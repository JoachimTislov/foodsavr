# Karma

Swedish surplus-food marketplace: restaurants, cafes and grocery stores list unsold food at a discount; customers reserve in the app. Founded 2015 in Stockholm. One article reports a 25% fee charged to the seller.

## Access

- **none** — no public API, developer program or unofficial client found. Consumer order history lives only in the app. A GDPR data request is the only likely export (unverified).

## Data

Offers (venue, bag description, price) and past purchases, in the app only.

## FoodSavr mapping

Possible deep link from expiring-item suggestions to nearby Karma offers; purchases are meals/bags, not inventory.

## Risks and constraints

No sanctioned access; scraping the app likely breaches terms (unverified).
