# Zepto

Zepto (quick commerce) has no public API. Only commercial or hobby scrapers for catalogue data. Zepto and Zomato-group MCP integrations were reported in press but not verified for Zepto.

## Access

- **bot** — hosted scrapers of product/price by location.

## Data

Prices, MRP, stock by pincode.

## FoodSavr mapping

Enrichment only; order history not reachable.

## Risks and constraints
