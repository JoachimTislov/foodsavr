# Welcome to the FoodSavr Documentation

This is the central hub for all documentation related to the FoodSavr project. Whether you're looking to contribute, understand the architecture, or just get the app running, this is the place to start.

`FoodSavr` is a little app to help you reduce food waste, save time, and manage your groceries. By tracking inventory and helping with meal plans, it makes sure you always know what you have and what you need.
