LettMat (formerly FoodSavr) · Test Environment Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This privacy notice covers the LettMat (formerly FoodSavr) test environment. It may contain real personal data. Norwegian law and the EU/EEA General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) apply.

Firebase handles sign-in and stores information such as your email address, inventory entries, shopping lists, collections, and preferences. LettMat uses this data to operate the app and test its features. Barcode lookups are sent to Open Food Facts, and receipt photos are processed on-device and deleted right after scanning. If you connect a supported grocery store in the mobile app, LettMat stores the connection tokens locally on your device.

Test data may be reset at any time. You can permanently delete your account and associated data at /account/delete (also reachable at /delete-account).
